Why we use it, and the lawful basis

  • To arrange and deliver your hire. Calling you to confirm the drive, taking the deposit, delivering and collecting the cube. Basis: taking steps at your request before a contract, then performing the contract.
  • To call you if a cube comes free. Only for the dates you gave us on the cancellation list. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To email you when we reach your area. One email when the round grows, not a newsletter. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep accounts and meet the law. Basis: legal obligation.
  • To keep the website secure and working. Basis: our legitimate interest in running a safe website.
  • To improve the site and measure our adverts. Google Analytics and Meta Pixel. Basis: your consent, given in the cookie banner, which you can withdraw at any time under “Cookie settings”.

We do not sell your data. Your name, phone number and email are never sent to Google or Meta. We do not make automated decisions about you. The postcode check only tells you whether you are inside our delivery area. A person confirms every booking by phone.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run the service, and only what they need:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the website and keeps short technical logs.
  • Resend delivers the booking, cancellation-list and out-of-area entries to our inbox by email.
  • Postcodes.io (run by Ideal Postcodes Ltd) looks up the postcode you type.
  • Google serves the fonts on this site. Your browser fetches them from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Google Ireland Ltd runs Google Analytics for us, only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd runs Meta Pixel, only if you accept marketing cookies. Meta may combine this with what it already knows about you if you use Facebook or Instagram, under its own privacy policy.
  • [PAYMENT PROVIDER] processes card payments.
  • [EMAIL AND PHONE PROVIDER] hosts our inbox and phone line.
  • [ACCOUNTANT] sees financial records when preparing our accounts.

We may also share data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

Data stored outside the UK

Vercel, Resend, Google and Meta may process data in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards UK law allows: the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum issued by the ICO.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • have it corrected if it is wrong;
  • have it deleted, unless we must keep it by law;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for the cancellation list, the out-of-area list, or cookies.

To use any of these, email drop@cubedrop.co.uk. We reply within one month. It is free.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
