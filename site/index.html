Before you commit

The two questions people actually ask.

Will it fit?

We need about 2.6 metres of clear width and ground that will take the weight. Gates, a slope, or a tight turn are worth a look. We check that on the call, before a date is firm.

Do I need permission?

A short let on a private drive is often allowed. Conservation streets are not a guess. We check your address before anything is delivered.

Questions

Before you reserve a date.

How much does it cost to hire a storage container?

From £50 a week, plus £149 for delivery and collection. Four weeks is £349 as a guide. We confirm the figure on the phone before anything is paid. The minimum hire is two weeks, and the longest we book here is 26 weeks.

What size is the storage container?

It is a 10ft storage container. You need about 2.6 metres of clear width. It is a compact store, not a 20ft box.

Do you deliver the storage container?

Yes. We place it on the drive and collect it when the hire ends. The earliest delivery day is a week ahead.

Can I use it as house move storage?

Yes. The unit stays on the drive so the house can empty, or fill, without a trip to a self storage warehouse.

Can I use it for storage during a renovation?

Yes. Book it for the weeks the work takes. Furniture and the things that cannot live in dust stay outside, locked.

Is it a shipping container?

It is a refurbished 10ft steel unit, painted black, with one bamboo bay by the door. It is built to be looked at. It is not a rusty shipping container and it is not a skip.

Will it fit on the drive, and do I need permission?

We need about 2.6 metres of clear width and ground that will take the weight. A short let on a private drive is often allowed. We check access and the address on the call, before a date is firm.
