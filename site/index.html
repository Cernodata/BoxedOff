Questions

The questions that decide it.

How much does the cube cost?

£100 a week. Delivery and collection are free, so four weeks is £400, not £400 plus a van. We confirm the figure with you before the balance is paid. One week is the minimum. 26 weeks is the longest we book here.

What size is the cube?

A secure 10ft cube, about 2.44 metres wide, 3 metres long and 2.6 metres high. Treatment against moisture and condensation is included. Shelving is optional.

How much storage do you actually get?

About 16 cubic metres inside. That is a one-bedroom flat: a sofa, a double bed, a washing machine and the boxes around them. It is not the contents of a whole house.

How wide a gap do you need to deliver the cube?

2.8 metres of clear width. The cube is 2.44 metres wide, plus a little either side so it can pass a gate or sit between a wall and a car. Measure the narrowest point on the way in, and the space it will stand in.

Does the drive have to be flat?

Yes. It has to sit flat and firm for its whole length. Concrete, tarmac, block paving or compacted stone. Not a slope, not a lawn, and not ground that will sink. The cube sits on a wooden frame, so it never rests on the floor and leaves no scratches or marks.

Are delivery and collection included?

Yes. We bring the cube to your door and collect it when the hire ends, and both are free. The earliest day is a week ahead. You don't tow it.

Can I use it as house move storage?

Yes. The cube stays on the drive while one house empties and the next fills. You are not driving to a warehouse each time you need something.

Can I use it for storage during a renovation?

Yes. Book the weeks the work takes. What can't live in the dust stays locked, outside. We collect the cube when the rooms are back.

Is this storage container hire?

Yes, with less faff. You hire a secure 10ft cube for the weeks you need, from £100 a week, and we deliver and collect it free. It is not a bare shipping container: it is treated so it stays dry, with shelving if you want it. And it is not a skip.

Can I add shelving?

Yes. Shelving is optional. Ask when you reserve, and we confirm it with the price.

Do I need permission for a cube on the drive?

A short let on a private drive is often allowed. A conservation street is not something to guess. We check the address on the call, before a date is firm.

How long can I hire the cube?

From 1 week to 26 weeks. Delivery and collection are free, and they are booked with the hire.

Can I get into the cube while it is on the drive?

Yes. It stays on your drive, locked, and it is yours until we collect it. Leave the door end clear. The doors open outwards.

How do I book?

Check the postcode, choose the weeks and a delivery day, and leave your name and number. A £50 deposit on the website reserves the cube. We then call to go through the details, and the balance follows that call. Or call 01565 662244.

What if the dates I want are taken?

We only keep a small number of cubes. Leave the day you need it, how many weeks, and a phone number on the cancellation list. If one comes free, we call, and a £50 deposit on the website reserves it.

Where do you deliver?

Knutsford, Alderley Edge, Wilmslow, Holmes Chapel, and about 12 km around the middle of those four. The postcode decides it, not the town name.
