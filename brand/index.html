Brand directions

BoxedOff · Three complete directions

Three companies you could actually run.

Not three logos. Three visual languages, each capable of carrying storage now and care and living later, without looking like a container yard, a skip firm or a welfare hire company.

Master name

One word. No gap. Capitals.

The useful thing about the name is that it is already a sentence ending. Renovating? BOXEDOFF. Need space? BOXEDOFF. That only works if the name is a single coined word, set like a stamp, not a description of what the product is.

A cube around the word is the first idea everyone has, and it traps the company inside storage. None of the three directions uses one. The master name stays BOXEDOFF even when the division changes.

Architecture

One brand. Three offers.

Master

BOXEDOFF is the only logo a neighbour needs to remember. Divisions never get their own symbols.

Divisions

STORAGE, CARE and LIVING sit under the wordmark in smaller tracked capitals. Same mark. Same colours. The word changes; the brand does not.

What stays off

No town names, no franchisee names, no padlocks, no medical crosses, no corrugated-metal clichés. High-visibility yellow is road kit, never a brand colour. It comes off at the customer’s door.

The unit as media

The quiet ask.

A unit outside a house for three months is advertising inventory the customer did not ask to host. Affluent driveways will not tolerate a billboard. The brand still has to be recognisable.

So the unit whispers and the truck speaks. Branding on a customer’s unit stays under eight percent of the elevation. A signature physical detail does the long-distance work. A small plate does the introduction. Curiosity, then a scan.

  • Distance A material or colour signature, not a slogan. Timber, kiln corner, or bamboo.
  • Eye height Mark and wordmark on a plate beside the door, no larger than a letterbox.
  • The ask One human line. “Need one?” or “Need some space?” Never a phone number in 200mm type.
  • The scan A QR code no bigger than 28mm, with boxedoff.uk set beside it for people who will not scan.
  • The truck This is where the name is allowed to be large. The arrival is the advertisement. The driveway is the proof.

01 · Architectural luxury

The Plan.

Specified, not hired. A floor-plan mark, a graphite body, a bronze jamb. The unit should look as if an architect drew it into the elevation of the house.

European. Timeless. Understated. For a £2 million home, and still dignified when the room inside is a bathroom.

Logo system

The mark is a room in plan, with the door left open.

Hairline geometry. The gap at the bottom is the entrance, not a missing piece of a cube. It engraves onto a 40mm bronze badge without falling apart. The wordmark is widely tracked, medium weight, never bold.

Signature · The Bronze Jamb

BOXEDOFF NEED ONE?

A laser-etched bronze plate, about 180 × 70mm, fixed at the door jamb. Above it, one warm downlight washing the door. Beside the door, a single 800mm bay of vertical timber-effect battens. Everything else is graphite paint. Corner castings stay. Corrugation stays, quieted by matt paint. This is a paint-and-plate job, not a rebuild. Competitors can copy a logo. They cannot casually copy a bronze jamb, a timber bay and a light that makes the door look designed.

On the driveway

Public face only. Plate at eye height. QR at 28mm, low, with the URL. Timber bay is battens on the existing wall, not a new structure.

Why this could win

The Plan is how BoxedOff gets specified rather than hired. A bronze jamb and a graphite body belong on a drive in Alderley Edge, and they give an architect nothing to object to. It is cheap to build and easy to police: one paint, one timber bay, one plaque. Care works because nothing about the unit looks medical. The weakness is memory. From a moving car it can pass as an expensive shed. Choose it to own the top of the market. Do not choose it if strangers need to ask what it is before anyone has introduced you.

02 · Disruptive consumer

The Stamp.

A boring industry, interrupted. Black, a kiln-orange corner, and a wordmark heavy enough to end a sentence.

Recognisable at a hundred metres. Built for film, social and the side of a truck. Personality without jokes.

Logo system

The frame is open. The problem has an exit.

Three sides of a square, thick, always open at the bottom. Not a cube, not a box around the word. The wordmark is the logo. The frame is the icon, the favicon, the crop on every film end-card. It never rotates.

Signature · The Kiln Corner

A 140mm stripe, full height, on the corner that faces the road. Vinyl or a masked paint edge. The open frame sits near the top, reversed out of the stripe. At the bottom, in small type: NEED ONE? plus a QR and boxedoff.uk. At dusk a small graze light catches the stripe so the corner glows. The rest of the unit is bare black. Large type is forbidden on the customer’s elevation. It is mandatory on the truck.

Why this could win

The Stamp is built to be noticed. A kiln corner is visible at a hundred metres, cheap for a franchisee to apply, and difficult to water down. The answer-line is a poster, a social post and a fifteen-second film, all at once. This is the fastest route to a national storage brand. It is also the hardest fit for someone coming home from hospital. Care can borrow the wordmark and quieten the colour, but the master brand will still feel loud in front of an insurer. Choose it if fame in storage matters more than an easy care pathway.

03 · Warm premium living

The Threshold.

A brand about coming in, not about boxes. The same object has to look right on an expensive drive and right when someone cannot use the bathroom upstairs.

Warm, calm, human. Natural materials. No healthcare blue, no crosses, no institutional greys.

Logo system

A doorway, open at the floor.

The mark is the proportion of a door, not a crate. Two jambs, a lintel, a ground line. You can walk through it. That idea covers storage, a temporary bathroom, and whatever Living becomes. The wordmark stays BOXEDOFF, set more gently than The Stamp and less cold than The Plan.

Signature · The Bamboo Bay

BOXEDOFF

A 600mm bay of bamboo-effect composite slats, full height, fixed to battens on the existing wall. A warm 2700K light hidden in the rebate, so the bay glows after dark. Why this could win

The Threshold is the only direction native to both products. Stone, a bamboo bay and a doorway look considered on a Cheshire drive, and like hospitality rather than welfare when the room is a bathroom. Solicitors, therapists and neighbours can all be shown the same picture. The mark means entry, not storage, so Care and Living do not need a rebrand. Franchisees inherit a material recipe. It will not shout like The Stamp. It earns “what’s that?” and then “that’s actually nice”, which is the reaction this business is built on.

Recommendation

Build The Threshold.

BoxedOff has to be desirable on an expensive driveway and appropriate for a fragile week at home. Those are not two brands. They are the same object, seen by a neighbour and by a case manager.

The Plan can do the driveway, and it can do care without causing offence, but it is easy to forget and easy to mistake for a garden building. The Stamp will be remembered, and it can build a national storage business quickly, but every care conversation starts with an apology for how loud the brand is. Trust is the harder thing to add later. Fame can be turned up on the truck, in film, and in the answer-line without painting the customer’s unit kiln orange.

The Threshold lets storage launch the company and lets Care and Living extend it without a new identity. The doorway is not a storage symbol, so the master brand does not have to be redesigned when the second product becomes half the business. Franchisees can be handed a short manual: warm greige shell, one bamboo bay, oak handle, olive and charcoal only, BOXEDOFF in one word, no town on the truck. That is enough to look like the same company in Knutsford and, later, in any other town.

Keep the answer-line. It belongs to the name, not only to The Stamp. Need space? BOXEDOFF. Say it calmly.
